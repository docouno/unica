## Purpose & Per-Theme Canvas Organization

`designs/themes/{soft,frost,pixel}/compositions/workspace-app.pen` (**Folio**) is the initial baseline composition in Unica's open `designs/themes/<theme>/compositions/<scene>.pen` set. It prototypes Unica's role-based design architecture across three distinct launch themes covering editorial productivity, expressive hardware-grade SaaS, and sharp-corner developer tooling from a single component API: **theme-owned surface physics and geometry**, **contextual surface adaptation (`base` vs `overlay`)**, and a **flat semantic token dictionary**. Once components ship in `packages/ui`, live interactive application compositions in `apps/playground` serve as the long-term visual and behavioral reference.

Splitting `.pen` canvases under `designs/themes/<theme>/{compositions,components}/` keeps each document fast (`20` frames = `Dark + Light × 10` scenes), isolates each theme slice cleanly, and avoids duplicated Markdown prose by maintaining this shared composition overview in `designs/compositions/workspace-app.md` (while normative public component contracts live in `docs/components/<family>.md`). All three canvases share **1:1 structural AST parity per column** with zero per-theme layout forks and zero raw `#hex` literals:

1. **`Soft` (`../themes/soft/compositions/workspace-app.pen`):**

## Semantic Token Dictionary & Code Mapping

[`unica.lib.pen`](../unica.lib.pen) serves as the single design-library source of truth with `unicaTheme = ["soft-dark", "soft-light", "frost-dark", "frost-light", "pixel-dark", "pixel-light"]` and `surfaceContext = ["base", "overlay"]`. In `packages/ui`, this maps directly to one structural base stylesheet plus six flat theme preset overrides (`soft-dark`, `soft-light`, `frost-dark`, `frost-light`, `pixel-dark`, `pixel-light`):

- **Surfaces:** `--bg-shell`, `--bg-aside`, `--bg-canvas`, `--bg-surface`, `--bg-surface-subtle`, `--bg-surface-raised`, `--bg-overlay`
- **Foregrounds:** `--fg-primary`, `--fg-secondary`, `--fg-muted`, `--fg-subtle`, `--fg-disabled`, `--fg-on-accent`
- **Borders:** `--border-subtle`, `--border-default`, `--border-strong`
- **Contextual Controls & Choice Cards (`surfaceContext: "base" | "overlay"`):** `--control-bg`, `--control-border`, `--control-border-hover`, `--choice-card-bg`, `--choice-card-bg-selected`, `--choice-card-border`, `--choice-card-border-selected`
- **Accent & Statuses:** `--accent-{solid,solid-hover,solid-active,soft-bg,soft-fg,border,signal}` and `--status-{info,success,warning,danger}-{solid,soft-bg,soft-fg,border,signal}`
- **Theme-Switched Geometry, Typography & Motifs:** `--radius-xs`, `--size-{s,m,l}-radius`, `--surface-{s,m,l}-radius`, `--radius-full` (all resolving to `0px` in `pixel-*`), `--font-family-base` (`Inter` in `soft-*` / `frost-*`; `JetBrains Mono` in `pixel-*`), and pure-CSS equivalents for `pixel-corner-ticks.glsl` and `pixel-grid.glsl`.

## Component Role, Shell & Surface Rules

1. **Composable `AppShell` Chrome (`TopRail`, `LeftRail`, `Aside`, `Stage`, `Header`, `Subbar`):**
   - **L-Shaped (`TopRail + LeftRail` + concentric `Stage` corner, scenes `02`–`06`):** `TopRail` (`46px`) owns the `56px` top-left brand cell; `AppShell.Stage` binds its top-left corner to `theme/shell/stage-outer-radius` / `theme/shell/stage-inner-radius` (`14px` / `13px` in `Soft` and `Frost`; `0px` in `Pixel`).
   - **Classic (`LeftRail + Aside + Main`, scenes `07`, `08`):** `LeftRail` hosts the brand mark and `Stage` uses a straight `1px` left seam.
   - **`Aside`-Only (`09`), `LeftRail`-Only (`10`), and Standalone (`01`):** Optional slots compose cleanly without per-screen layout wrappers.
2. **Role-Based Buttons (`Button`):** Public roles `variant="primary" | "secondary" | "ghost"` (mapped on the library's `solid` / `outline` / `ghost` theme recipes) crossed with `tone="accent" | "neutral" | "danger"`. Semantic status hues (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`) belong exclusively to passive status markers and feedback (`Badge`, `Alert`, `Toast`, `Indicator`).
3. **Contextual Surface Adaptation (`surfaceContext: "base" | "overlay"`):** On `base` surfaces, controls and `*Card` choices use structured recessed plates; inside `overlay` containers (`[data-surface="overlay"]`), `Frost` switches controls to translucent glass veils while `Soft` and `Pixel` apply stepped opaque overlay plates.
4. **Static Composition Cleanliness:** Open overlay triggers and dialog containers render without spurious static focus rings (`inputFocus="none"`, `dialogDirectFocus="off"`). `SegmentedControl` keeps consistent figure-ground polarity (dark active thumb in Dark presets, light active thumb in Light presets), and continuous tracks align 1:1 across `Progress`, `Slider`, and `RangeSlider`.
